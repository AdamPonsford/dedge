Luck Cap & Shovel Advisory

Advice on exact luck caps to lock at so higher rarity minerals don't override lower tier drops.

Do NOT over-luck low tiers
Essential Prospecting Mechanics Rules:
  • Lower Rarity / Common / Uncommon / Rare: High luck dilutes and removes Common/Rare loot slots! Switch to an Items Farming Build and lock your luck at the indicated max limit (often 10, 25, 50, 250, 500, or 2.5k).
  • Excavating Shovel: Provides optimal digging chances for low tiers without pushing into ultra-rare loot pools.
  • Epic Ores (†): For most Epic ores, lower digging quality is better. Use a shovel with an enchant other than Mastered (e.g. Tempered, Excavating, etc.).
  • Legendary / Mythic / Exotic: Always run Luck Efficiency with no luck lock (uncapped) and activate relics for corresponding weather events.
